Six ways to tell
your product story.

UGC-style ads use an AI presenter to speak about your product. Pick a story template, then change the words.

STORY TEMPLATE 01

Product introduction

Introduce a product and invite viewers to explore.

Meet a simple step for your daily routine.
Introduce→Product→Invite
STORY TEMPLATE 02

Product details

Focus on the details people should notice.

Let’s take a closer look at the headphones.
Attention→Details→Invite
STORY TEMPLATE 03

Question and answer

Ask a useful question and explain the answer.

Looking for a new coffee for your morning?
Ask→Answer→Invite
STORY TEMPLATE 04

Simple explainer

Explain the product in straightforward language.

Here is a simple introduction to the cream.
Context→Explain→Next step
STORY TEMPLATE 05

New product launch

Announce the product and show what it offers.

Meet the next pair for your daily run.
Announce→Introduce→Invite
STORY TEMPLATE 06

Product discovery

Give the audience a reason to take a closer look.

Discover a new part of your morning routine.
Discover→Details→Explore

All six story structures are included. Example scripts and visual pairings were created for this listing.
